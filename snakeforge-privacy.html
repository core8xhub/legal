How this game handles your data

Who we are. CoreX Solution, contact: admin@core8x.com.

What we collect. When you play, the game records pseudonymous gameplay statistics: a random player number (created on your device — it is not your name, email or any account), which in-game choices you made and when (including the real-world time of each event and the time our server received it), when you restarted, and your answers to two short in-game questions. Gameplay records do not contain your IP address, name, email, device ID or portal account ID. Our server temporarily uses the connection IP address, in memory only, for per-minute abuse protection without writing it into any gameplay record; hosting and network providers may process connection metadata as described under Processors.

Why. To learn whether the game is understandable and fun — for example, whether players get better at its central choice over time. The game shows one of two versions of a game rule, picked at random, so we can tell which version plays better. We do this based on our legitimate interest in measuring and improving our own game; you can object at any time (see Your rights), and the measurement is designed to work without knowing who you are.

Where it goes. Statistics are sent over an encrypted connection to our collection server, hosted in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur, operated for us by Hostinger), where they are held briefly before being stored in a locked data store in the EU (Frankfurt, Amazon Web Services). Our hosting providers act as processors: Hostinger (collection server, Kuala Lumpur) and Amazon Web Services (storage, eu-central-1 Frankfurt). Amazon Web Services processes the stored data under its data-processing addendum, which is part of its service terms, with storage pinned to the Frankfurt region. The corresponding agreement covering the Hostinger collection hold — including the transfer safeguards for the brief hold in Malaysia — is not yet in place as of this notice's date. This is not left to good intentions: the collection server refuses to start in live measurement mode until we record the accepted agreement, so a measurement period cannot open while this gap exists. This notice will be updated when the agreement is in place. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or combined with other data about you.

How long. An immutable evidence copy of your gameplay records is technically locked for 60 days: its stored versions cannot be changed or deleted during that period — by anyone, including us — and any attempt to mask them with additions is detected during our verification step. Temporary server copies are deleted when the measurement period closes. Our working copy is deleted no later than 60 days after the measurement period ends. Storage-provider deletion of the evidence copy becomes eligible on day 61 and is then carried out by the provider's automatic lifecycle process. After deletion, only aggregate statistics and integrity hashes remain.

Your rights. Press I on the game's start screen to see your player number (displayed so you can transcribe it). Send it to admin@core8x.com to ask what data exists for it (access), to correct it (rectification), to restrict its processing, or to object to this measurement. Because the store is tamper-locked, individual records cannot be plucked out early — deletion happens for everyone, automatically, after the 60-day period. Clearing this site's browser data removes the locally stored player number; on a later visit the game creates a new number, so new activity cannot be linked to the old one — this does not delete records already received. If you are in the EU/EEA, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Changes. If we ever collect anything beyond the list above, this notice will change BEFORE that happens, and the game's version counter makes old and new data separable.
